LENS score

LENS: Liver Evaluation and Navigation for Screening. LENS score = LENS-Base probability × 100 (range 0–100).

For adults with cardiometabolic risk. LENS adds assessment for women; FIB-4 applies to women and men. The model estimates MASLD with elevated liver stiffness (MASLD-ELS: CAP ≥288 dB/m and LSM ≥8 kPa).

Reference values are study assessment thresholds, not diagnostic cutoffs. Calculations run locally; entered values are not stored or transmitted.
